## Data-quality policy applied

- Calculations use `userFillsByTime` with `aggregateByTime: true`; a non-aggregated pass checks the 10,000-fill availability ceiling for wallets that otherwise meet the episode count.
- Final ranking is cumulative 30D PnL divided by the maximum net outside capital committed during the window. Deposits and withdrawals are removed from the normalized equity curve, so post-withdrawal dust cannot inflate returns.
- Deep-screen coverage is the first 500 eligible leaderboard wallets ordered by official 30D ROI; the report does not claim coverage outside that declared candidate pool.
- Wallets with fewer than 10 completed position episodes are excluded; partial fills never count as separate investments.
- Portfolio MDD uses Hyperliquid's PnL/account-value drawdown formula and therefore includes open-position equity changes.
- Carry-in and still-open positions are excluded only from the completed-episode count and completed net PnL diagnostic.
- Completed spot episodes or completed episodes with non-USDC fees are marked UNVERIFIABLE because their net PnL cannot be compared exactly from the required fields.
- Funding and unrealized PnL remain excluded from completed net PnL, but are represented in official portfolio ROI/MDD.
